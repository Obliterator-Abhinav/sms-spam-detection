02 / Problem + data

Can a model tell spam
from a real message?

We frame SMS filtering as a binary classification problem: spam vs. ham (normal).

The problem

One inbox. Two very different messages.

HAM

“Hey, are we meeting at 5 today?”

SPAM

“You won a prize! Call now to claim…”

Goal: catch unwanted messages without mistaking genuine texts for spam.

05 / Learnings + ownership

What we learned —
and who owned each part

A useful classifier is more than a high accuracy score: it needs reliable data handling and honest evaluation.

Key learnings
01
Text needs numbers

CountVectorizer turns words into features the model can use.

02
Class imbalance matters

Precision, recall and F1 reveal more than accuracy alone.

03
Evaluation must be honest

The model missed 8 spam messages; test performance is below training F1.

04
Data has limits

2011 English SMS may not represent today's Indian spam, OTP and Hinglish scams.
